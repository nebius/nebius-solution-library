### Serverless AI

[Serverless AI](./serverless-ai/README.md)

Run containerized AI workloads without managing long-lived VM fleets or clusters. The primary examples are a small Qwen LoRA fine-tuning Job with Axolotl and a vLLM support-ticket triage Endpoint. Minimal `nvidia-smi` and nginx quick checks are included for validating quota, networking, logs, and token auth.
